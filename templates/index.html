About the project

Technology with a Purpose

Smart Food Reduction is a college innovation project designed to support better food management and reduce avoidable waste.

Our Mission

The project records prepared and consumed food, calculates surplus, estimates future demand from previous records, evaluates recipient capacity, and stores redistribution details.

Its goal is to encourage responsible food planning and make surplus food easier to track and manage.

Technology Stack

HTML and CSS Website structure and Premium Technology design
Python and Flask Application logic and form processing
SQLite Persistent food and redistribution records
